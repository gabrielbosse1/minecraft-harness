PHASED BUILDING — always bottom-up, one named phase per call:
1. foundation — level the footprint, lay the base course.
2. walls_with_windows — raise the shell, leave the openings.
3. roof — cap it (stairs/slabs), then stop and reassess.
4. interior_details — doors, light, furniture, decoration.
Use build_shape or a blueprint for anything larger than a detail; never place a big build block by block. Name the phase in every build call's stage. Re-running a phase is safe and idempotent — resume rather than rebuild. Stop and reassess after each phase.
